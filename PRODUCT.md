# UniMatch

## Platform
Web, Vietnamese, desktop and mobile.

## Users and purpose
Students and thesis coordinators prepare student profiles, discover supervisors from approved evidence, and review quota-constrained allocation proposals.

## Stack and constraints
Existing JavaScript modules, Three.js and FastAPI. PPO and DQN are primary models; Hungarian is a named baseline. Preserve CSV student validation, equal quotas, review and export. Faculty catalog is managed outside the student UI. Do not infer academic accuracy or personal proficiency from compatibility scores.

## Approved experience
Social sign-in and registration with Google, GitHub and Facebook, Cloudflare Turnstile, then a soft ocean-blue animated 3D landing page. Friendly student workspace, balanced centered actions, clear comparison, cute explanatory 3D replay, training dashboard and thesis-only cited chatbot. Provider credentials remain deployment configuration.
