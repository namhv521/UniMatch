# UniMatch portal

The approved visual world is a bright ocean-blue campus. Manrope is self-hosted. Navy text, pale blue page surfaces and white task areas preserve contrast; orange and green distinguish DQN and Hungarian in charts.

Login uses a two-column welcome scene and social-authentication panel. Landing pairs a large headline with a native Three.js campus, cute students and supervisors. Workspace follows student import, faculty selection, comparison, 3D replay and cited chat. Training is a separate readable page with real curves, uncertainty bands, selected steps and plateau criteria.

Actions are centered within their task groups. Inputs have visible labels. Focus rings and minimum 44px primary targets support keyboard and touch use. At 900px the workspace becomes one column; at 600px actions stack and navigation wraps. Wide result tables scroll inside their own container.

Three.js scenes pause for reduced-motion preferences, offscreen elements and hidden tabs. Users can toggle motion. HTML decision text and load bars remain the accessible authority. Replay shows up to six participating supervisors, always including the active destination; it never represents the decorative landing scene as real data.

All visual models use native Three.js geometry, generated in code; no downloaded or generated raster assets ship. QA screenshots are local verification artifacts, not application assets.
